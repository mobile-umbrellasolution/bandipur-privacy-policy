Privacy Policy

Bandipur Cable Car Agent App — by Umbrella Solution

✅

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the Bandipur Cable Car Agent Ticketing App, including how we handle device permissions for PDF downloads and notifications.

🛡️
Information We Collect

When you register and use the Bandipur Cable Car Agent Ticketing App, we collect the following information:

  • Full name, business name (Agent Name), and contact person details
  • Email address and mobile number
  • Business address and PAN/VAT number
  • Profile picture (optional)
  • Bank account or digital wallet details for commission disbursement
  • Ticket transaction records, including passenger details entered during sales
  • Device information and usage logs for security and performance monitoring
📊
How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Create and manage your agent account
  • Process ticket sales and issue valid tickets to customers
  • Calculate and disburse agent commissions to your registered payment method
  • Communicate important updates, policy changes, or account notifications
  • Send download-complete notifications when PDFs are saved to your device
  • Monitor and prevent fraudulent activity on the platform
  • Improve the performance and reliability of the App
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations under Nepali law
📥
PDF Downloads & File Storage

The App allows you to download ticket invoices and boarding tickets as PDF documents. Here is how file storage works:

  • On Android, PDFs are saved to the Downloads/BandipurCableCar/ folder on your device's external storage. This requires storage access permission.
  • On iOS, PDFs are shared via the system share sheet. No files are written directly by the App — you choose where to save them.
  • Downloaded files remain on your device and are not uploaded back to our servers. We have no access to files stored on your device.
  • File names include the bill number for easy identification, for example: invoice-BILL001.pdf.
  • We do not track which files you download or how long you keep them on your device.
🔔
Notification Permissions

On Android, the App uses local device notifications to confirm when a PDF has been successfully saved to your device.

  • Notifications are local only — generated by the App on your device and never sent through an external push server for download confirmations.
  • On Android 13+ (API 33+), the App will request POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission. You may decline without affecting PDF downloads.
  • Tapping a download notification opens the saved PDF in your device's default PDF viewer using a secure content URI.
  • You can disable App notifications at any time in Settings → Apps → BandipurCableCar → Notifications.
  • We do not use notifications for advertising or marketing purposes.
🌐
Information Sharing

Bandipur Cable Car Ltd. does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • With banking or wallet service providers to process commission payments
  • With government authorities or regulators when required by law
  • With technology service providers who assist in operating the App, under strict confidentiality agreements
  • In the event of a business merger or acquisition, where data may be transferred to the successor entity
🔒
Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), secure storage of sensitive information such as PAN/VAT and payment details, access controls to limit internal data access, and regular security monitoring. PDF documents downloaded to your device are protected only by your device's own security — we recommend enabling screen lock and device encryption. No digital system is entirely free from risk. We encourage agents to use strong passwords and keep their credentials confidential.

🕐
Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your agent account is active or as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy. Transaction records are retained for a minimum period as required by Nepali financial regulations. Upon account termination, data may be retained for an additional period for audit and legal compliance purposes before being securely deleted. Files downloaded to your device are not subject to our retention policy — they remain on your device until you delete them.

✋
Your Rights

As an agent, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Update your commission payment details through the App or by contacting support
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
  • Revoke notification permission at any time via your device settings without affecting core App functionality
  • Revoke storage permission at any time via your device settings, understanding this will disable the PDF download feature
📱
Third-Party Services

The App may integrate with third-party payment providers (such as eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, or banking institutions) to facilitate ticket payments and commission disbursements. These services operate under their own privacy policies. Bandipur Cable Car Ltd. is not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party providers. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

⚠️
Device Permissions Summary

The App requests the following device permissions and uses them solely as described:

  • Storage (Android) — To save PDF invoices and tickets to your Downloads folder. Never used to access your personal photos or other files.
  • Notifications — To confirm when a PDF download is complete and allow tap-to-open. Never used for marketing.
  • Location — For operational and security purposes per Bandipur Cable Car guidelines.
  • Camera / Media — For profile picture upload only.
🔄
Changes to This Policy

Bandipur Cable Car Ltd. may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When material changes are made, agents will be notified through the App or via registered email. Continued use of the App after such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact Bandipur Cable Car Ltd. through your designated agent support channel or visit our registered office in Nepal.
